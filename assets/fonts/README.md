# Bundled fonts

English uses Lato: regular (400), bold (700), italic (400), and bold italic (700).
Arabic uses Noto Naskh Arabic, preserving connected shaping and diacritics.
The fonts are bundled and do not depend on device-installed fonts or a runtime
font download.

Downloaded from the Google Fonts repository:
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lato
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notonaskharabic

Unmodified fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Full licenses are in Lato-OFL.txt and NotoNaskhArabic-OFL.txt alongside the fonts;
both license texts are also included in Flutter's asset bundle.
