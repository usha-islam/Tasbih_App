# Reading content review

Reviewed 2026-10-15. The app is a manual counting aid. It does not calculate
prayer times, reset at midnight, or claim that its session lists are exhaustive.
English meanings are short explanatory renderings, not verbatim published
translations. Transliterations are reading aids, not a substitute for Arabic.

## Sources and scope

| Entries | Source checked | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ayatul Kursi | [Quran 2:255](https://quran.com/2/255) | Complete Arabic in standard spelling, with diacritics and pause marks. The existing Fajr/Maghrib placement and one-recitation goal are retained as session organization, not a restriction to those prayers. No unverified full transliteration or translation was added. |
| Astaghfirullah | [Sahih Muslim 591](https://sunnah.com/muslim:591) | Three repetitions after prayer; Arabic, simple transliteration and meaning included. |
| SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar | [Sahih Muslim 596a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:596a) | The 33/33/34 post-prayer sequence is supported here. Other established sequences exist; this is not claimed to be the only one. |
| La ilaha illallah; the longer wahdahu la sharika lah formula | [Sahih al-Bukhari 6403](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6403) | Short entry is the opening declaration; longer entry matches the full formula. The source's 100/day context is not imposed on personal counting. |
| La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah | [Sahih al-Bukhari 6384](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6384) | Arabic and plain meaning reviewed; no fixed personal count prescribed. |
| SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi; SubhanAllahil Azim; combined entry | [Sahih al-Bukhari 6405](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6405), [6406](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6406) | Individual expressions and combined wording reviewed. The 100/day report concerns the former phrase; arbitrary personal targets do not inherit that recommendation. |
| La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu minaz-zalimin | [Quran 21:87](https://quran.com/21/87) | Supplication excerpt, not the full verse; Arabic and meaning reviewed. |
| Astaghfirullahal Azim ... wa atubu ilayh | [Jami at-Tirmidhi 3577](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3577), [Abu Dawud 1517](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1517) | Tirmidhi includes al-Azim; Abu Dawud's wording omits that epithet. No fixed count imposed. |
| Ya Hayyu Ya Qayyum, bi rahmatika astagheeth | [Jami at-Tirmidhi 3524](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3524) | Wording and meaning reviewed. The displayed source grades this Hasan (Darussalam); no claim of unanimous grading. |
| Allahumma salli ala Muhammad; ... wa ala ali Muhammad | [Sahih al-Bukhari 3370](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3370) | These are abbreviated openings of the longer salawat formula, not its full text. No special count or reward claimed for the abbreviations. |
| Allahumma salli wa sallim ala nabiyyina Muhammad | [Quran 33:56](https://quran.com/33/56) | General invocation consistent with the instruction to send blessings and peace. This exact wording was not verified as a distinct narrated formula; no special attribution or count is claimed. |
| Allahumma ajirni minan-nar | [Abu Dawud 5079](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:5079) | The source grades the seven-repetition report weak (Al-Albani). The app explicitly labels seven as an optional personal goal and displays the qualification. |

Home and Dhikr List targets (33, 100, custom, free count) are **personal counting
goals**. Custom daily entries are also personal goals. Verified daily-sequence
context is shown separately from the counter's numeric target.

The review checked the above texts and references, not every scholarly chain
assessment or recitation tradition. Arabic uses ordinary Unicode spelling rather
than a particular printed mushaf's glyph encoding. A qualified Arabic/Quran
reader should still review the presentation before public distribution.
